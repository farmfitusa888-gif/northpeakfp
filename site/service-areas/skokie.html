Registering a business in Skokie

Every village on the North Shore sets its own rule, and they diverge more than most owners expect.

Skokie takes the opposite approach to Evanston on the question that matters most to small operators: most home-based businesses do not need a village licence, with childcare operations the notable exception. For businesses that do need one, licences expire on April 30 each year.

Property assessment in Skokie

Worth knowing before you assume the rules are the same everywhere in the north suburbs. They are not.

Skokie is assessed by the Cook County Assessor, and it sits in the north suburban township group — reassessed in 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025, which puts the next one in 2028. In a reassessment year there are two chances to contest a value; in an off year, generally one. How the Cook County appeal cycle works →

Common questionsSkokie
Q

Do I need a business licence to operate in Skokie?

Skokie takes the opposite approach to Evanston on the question that matters most to small operators: most home-based businesses do not need a village licence, with childcare operations the notable exception. For businesses that do need one, licences expire on April 30 each year.

Q

Which county and township handle assessment for a Skokie business?

Skokie is in Cook County, Niles Township. That decides who values your property, which appeal window applies, and who you call first — and it is not the same answer a few miles away.
