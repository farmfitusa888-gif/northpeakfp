Registering a business in Evanston

Every village on the North Shore sets its own rule, and they diverge more than most owners expect.

Evanston is one of the broadest in the area: every for-profit business operating within the city must register, explicitly including home-based occupations and businesses already licensed under state or federal law. Registration renews annually by April 1, and the city states a penalty of $500 for each day a business operates unregistered — which makes this one of the more expensive things to overlook in the north suburbs.

Property assessment in Evanston

Worth knowing before you assume the rules are the same everywhere in the north suburbs. They are not.

Evanston is assessed by the Cook County Assessor, and it sits in the north suburban township group — reassessed in 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025, which puts the next one in 2028. In a reassessment year there are two chances to contest a value; in an off year, generally one. How the Cook County appeal cycle works →
