Registering a business in Morton Grove

Every village on the North Shore sets its own rule, and they diverge more than most owners expect.

Morton Grove is among the most comprehensive: all industrial, commercial and home-based businesses are required to hold an active licence and renew it annually, on a licence year running January 1 to December 31. The village is explicit that the licence is a compliance check — it verifies the business against local zoning, building, health and fire codes, not just its existence.

Property assessment in Morton Grove

Worth knowing before you assume the rules are the same everywhere in the north suburbs. They are not.

Morton Grove is assessed by the Cook County Assessor, and it sits in the north suburban township group — reassessed in 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025, which puts the next one in 2028. In a reassessment year there are two chances to contest a value; in an off year, generally one. How the Cook County appeal cycle works →
